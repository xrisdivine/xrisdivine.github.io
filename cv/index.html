Profile

Product R&D leader with a track record across Meta Reality Labs, Microsoft Research, and Apple — each time entering at early research and driving it to production. Operating thesis, laid out in my essay “Evaluation Is the Product”: with nondeterministic systems, the only way to genuinely center the human is to measure — every fuzzy interaction goal has to become a concrete, repeatable signal before it can ship. A PhD in Human-Centered Computing is training for defining what “good” means and knowing what feels right from an interaction perspective. Collaborate daily with ML leads across data taxonomy, data generation, model integration, and persona definition — including curating the voice and language datasets that made expressive voices genuinely expressive — and with engineering leads to ensure those integrations land well in product, from application latency to on-device performance. Three patents on AI interactivity, more pending.
