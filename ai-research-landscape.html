Section 04

Cross-Cutting Themes, Tensions & Debates

The most important dynamics shaping the field emerge at the intersection of all three domains.

Emerging Themes Across Domains

Models + HCI + Voice

Agents Going Mainstream

MCP standardization, Claude Code, LangGraph in production. UIST papers on monitoring/steering agents. Voice becoming an agentic interface. The full stack is converging on autonomous AI agents.

HCI + Voice

From Tools to Teammates to Mediators

CHI 2025 Best Papers feature AI as intergroup mediator and delegated communicator. Voice agents handle calls on your behalf. The metaphor is shifting from tool to social actor.

Models + Voice

The Codec Language Model Paradigm

Treating speech as discrete tokens and applying LLM architectures (VALL-E, Dia, Moshi). The same transformer scaling that powers text now powers speech.

Models

Reasoning as a Product Category

o1/o3, DeepSeek-R1, Claude extended thinking created a distinct tier. Users trade latency/cost for accuracy. Inference-time compute may be the primary axis of improvement.

Models + HCI

Small Models Getting Smarter

Phi-4 (14B) beating GPT-4 on math. Distilled reasoning models. On-device deployment. Accessibility as killer app for small, local models.

HCI

Cultural & Value Plurality

Universal AI design is dead. Writing homogenization, dialect bias, cross-cultural trust all point to culture-specific AI interaction design.

Voice

Full-Duplex Dialogue

Moving beyond half-duplex to simultaneous processing. Moshi pioneered dual-stream; GPT-4o and Gemini Live implement variants. From turn-taking to true conversation.

Models + Voice

Multimodal Unification

Models becoming natively multimodal rather than bolting on modalities. GPT-4o's unified architecture, video with native audio. The modular pipeline era is ending.

HCI + Models

Capabilities Over Accuracy

Shift from "did the AI get it right?" to "did the AI expand what people can do?" Reframing evaluation around human empowerment, not task completion.

Key Tensions & Debates

01

Open vs. Closed Weights

DeepSeek-R1 showed open models match frontier closed models. HCI's reproducibility crisis is directly linked to studying closed models. Safety implications cut both ways.

02

Automation vs. Augmentation

Copilot creates new costs (50% time inspecting/repairing). Most "collaborative" systems are automation with a thin human veneer. Voice agents escalate the same question.

03

Safety vs. Capabilities Race

FLI Safety Index shows gap between ambition and safeguards. RSPs and ASL frameworks accelerate, but so do capabilities. The race between governance and capability continues.

04

The Explanation Paradox

Explanations increase acceptance regardless of correctness (Bansal, CHI 2021). Only help for hard tasks (Vasconcelos, CSCW 2023). When do explanations help vs. hurt?

05

Creativity: Catalyst or Crutch?

AI reduces divergent thinking (CHI 2024) but enhances as lateral inspiration (CHI 2025). Resolution is design-dependent: AI-as-starting-point harms; AI-as-lateral-input helps.

06

Voice Cloning: Utility vs. Ethics

3-second cloning enables accessibility and deepfake fraud. ELVIS Act, EU AI Act, TAKE IT DOWN Act respond. Consent frameworks lag technology.

07

Modular Pipelines vs. End-to-End

ASR→LLM→TTS is debuggable but lossy. End-to-end is faster and richer but harder to audit. Production uses pipelines; research moves toward unified.

08

Pre-training Scale vs. Inference Compute

Evidence of pre-training plateaus. o1/o3 and R1 show inference compute substitutes. Profound implications for deployment economics and accessibility.

Where the Domains Converge

Alignment Meets Interaction Design

Constitutional AI and RLHF are technical alignment; trust calibration and explanation design are interaction alignment. These communities are converging.

The Evaluation Crisis

ML benchmarks saturate. HCI user studies don't scale. Audio has no conversational quality standard. All three need new methods — the HEAL workshop bridges them.

Agent Architectures Need UX

ML builds capable agents; HCI shows capability without controllability creates unusable systems. Voice adds real-time. How do humans maintain control over autonomous AI?

Accessibility as Killer App

LLMs enable fundamentally new accessibility interactions: NL computer control for blind users, real-time scene description, automated auditing. Voice is transformative for non-visual interfaces.

The Reproducibility Problem

HCI studies closed models. Audio depends on proprietary APIs. ML benchmarks are gamed. All three build on proprietary, shifting foundations. Open weights help, but the gap persists.
